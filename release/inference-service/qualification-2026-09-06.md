It overlays only `xpc_entry.py`, `inference_workspace.py`, `muesli_backend.py`, and `reprocess.py` from this proof branch, whose baseline is `bc702fa`. An expected lock/source record is not a claim that those dependencies are loaded or production-qualified.

PATH remains the one bundled tools directory. No general shell or system-tool delegation is added.
